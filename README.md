# Create: Genesis Modpack

![genesis_title_scene](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/36b73088-8515-44c7-b891-515ca156670a)

Welcome to Create: Genesis — a modpack built from the ground up for players who love the simple creativity of vanilla Minecraft, but want to see its full potential.  This pack adds new ways to travel, fight, explore, and build, all with the goal of keeping things simple, balanced, and beginner-friendly.  And, at its center, is the Create mod, bringing steampunk-style machines and unique contraptions to life!  Perfect for solo worlds or servers, for engineers or builders, for peaceful farmers or battle-hungry combatants — this pack has something for everyone!

***

## Highlights
### Built for Vanilla Fans
Every mod and feature was carefully chosen to tranform the experience of Minecraft, without losing its heart.  Still the same game you love, just with more tools in your toolbox!
### Beginner-Friendly
Create has its own in-game Ponder menu, but not all mods are so detailed.  Genesis includes customized tooltips and added JEI info to fill in the gaps, making it easy to understand new content!  (A full in-game guidebook is planned for future updates.)
### Create-Focused Automation
With Create and many of its add-ons, the possibilities are endless!  Almost everything in the game can be automated, though you might find yourself paying a hefty price to produce high-value items.  With favorite add-ons like Create Crafts & Additions, Create: Steam 'n' Rails, Create: Enchantable Machinery, and Create: Enchantment Industry, the only limit is your imagination!
### Rich Build Palettes
Chipped, Rechiseled, and Macaw's suite of mods (and more!) equip the builders out there to exercise their gifts — the right block for the job is always close at hand.  Also included is Create: Copycats+, allowing decorative incorporation of almost any block texture in both aesthetic and practical uses!
### Better Exploration
Sail the seas with the Small Ships mod, or take to the skies with Immersive Aircraft!  If you'd prefer to keep your feet on solid ground, you can still enjoy the scenic views of Terralith's gorgeous terrain travelling by horse or train!  You can even haul everything but the kitchen sink (and probably that too) along with you using Sophisticated Backpacks.
### Advanced Combat
With the addition of MrCrayfish's Gun Mod (re-stylized by the Create Guns texture pack), Create Big Cannons, and Create: Radars, combat can be taken to a whole new level.  For you pacifists, fear not!  PVP Flagging and Open Parties & Claims ensure that no one is disturbed if they would rather enjoy their game in peace.
### Play with Friends
Designed for our own Create: Genesis SMP, this modpack also includes social features like Simple Voice Chat and Showcase Item to enjoy the game alongside your fellow miners and crafters!
